# Skill: STRICT PHOTO EDITING MODE (Retoque Fotográfico de Alta Fidelidad)

**Contexto:** Utilizar en modelos de IA generativa (Image-to-Image) para mejorar las fotografías de los platos (croquetas, berenjenas, huevos rotos, torreznos) y bebidas de la Taberna del Colono sin alterar su esencia, manteniendo los tonos de madera, el ambiente cálido y tradicional.

**Prompt Maestro para Comidas:**
```text
STRICT PHOTO EDITING MODE

Use the uploaded image as the only source.
DO NOT generate a new image.

Preserve exactly:
- original food (especially traditional tapas like croquetas, torreznos)
- original plate
- original table
- original background
- original perspective
- original framing
- original composition
- original food arrangement

Do not:
- add ingredients
- remove ingredients
- replace ingredients
- restyle the dish
- redesign plating
- modify the table
- modify the background
- modify furniture
- change camera angle

Apply only professional food photography retouching:
+ local contrast
+ micro contrast
+ texture detail
+ sharpness
+ highlight recovery
+ shadow recovery
+ color accuracy
+ dynamic range

Improve:
- crispness
- food texture (e.g. crunchiness of fried tapas)
- freshness
- visual depth
- appetizing appearance
- warm, golden hues typical of traditional Spanish cooking

Reduce:
- excessive yellow cast
- blown highlights
- muddy shadows
- image noise

Maintain:
- authentic rustic restaurant/tavern atmosphere
- original colors
- original wood tones
- warm ambient lighting

Professional Lightroom editing.
The final image must look like the same photograph professionally edited by a food photographer.

Image fidelity: 95%.
No scene reconstruction.
No object replacement.
No generative changes.
```

Preserve:
- original bar atmosphere
